## Launch boundary

No candidate session may start until a disposable execution environment has a
fresh, independently reviewed receipt proving all of the following:

- the candidate sees exactly one frozen fixture and its answer schema, never a
  held-out key, another fixture, host home or benchmark control file;
- authentication is available to the Codex parent but absent from candidate
  files, inherited environment and readable process state before model tool
  execution;
- candidate shell/network policy is active and fail closed;
- numeric launch, wall, retry, failure and aggregate cost policies are bound to
  the current preregistration, with token, turn and tool telemetry explicitly
  descriptive rather than falsely presented as an enforceable cap;
- cancellation leaves no surviving candidate process; and
- the disposable runtime and exact inputs can be reconstructed without a
  provider or host-home dependency.

Failed macOS Seatbelt, Docker/lower-layer and nested-sandbox probes are control
evidence only. They do not authorize a weaker boundary and are not candidate
records. The current candidate denominator remains zero until the benchmark
owner freezes the new runtime receipt, independent review passes and launch
authorization binds both.
